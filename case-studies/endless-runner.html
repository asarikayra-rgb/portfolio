The Problem / Goal

Create a fast-paced 3D endless runner with responsive controls, readable obstacles and progressively increasing difficulty. The goal was to make gameplay challenging while maintaining fair obstacle placement and smooth player movement.

The project focused on combining movement mechanics, random obstacle spawning, collectibles and a rewarding score-based progression system.

My Role: Game Design, C# Gameplay Programming, Player Movement, Obstacle Systems, UI/UX Design, Scoring and Game Flow.

Key Features

Lane-Based Movement Three-lane movement system with keyboard and touch-based controls.
Jump Mechanics Jump functionality with ground detection for platform navigation.
Dynamic Obstacle Spawning Random lane-based obstacle spawning to create gameplay variation.
Coin Collection Collectible coins with trigger-based detection and scoring.
Camera Follow Follow camera system to maintain a consistent gameplay view.
Progressive Difficulty Increasing game speed and challenge to keep gameplay engaging.
Score & High Score Score tracking and persistent high-score storage using PlayerPrefs.
Revive System A simulated rewarded-ad revive flow to give players another chance.
Game Over & Retry Game-over interface with score display and restart functionality.

Design Highlights

  • Designed lane-based movement to make navigation simple and easy to understand.
  • Used random lane-based obstacle spawning to create unpredictable gameplay.
  • Focused on balancing forward speed and obstacle placement.
  • Introduced collectible coins to encourage continued player engagement.
  • Designed a score and high-score system to encourage replayability.
  • Created a clear Game Over and retry flow to reduce unnecessary navigation.
  • Included a simulated revive flow as part of the monetization design.

Playtest & Iteration

During development, I encountered several movement, physics and collision-related challenges while testing the gameplay.

  • Player Movement & Vibration: The character experienced unwanted movement and vibration due to conflicts between Transform-based movement and Rigidbody physics. I investigated separating movement behaviour from physics-based actions.
  • Obstacle Collision: The player sometimes passed through obstacles at higher movement speeds. Reducing forward speed from 8 to 2 improved the behaviour, but collision tunnelling still needed further tuning.
  • Player Getting Stuck: The character could become stuck inside obstacles, affecting movement and jumping. I investigated collider settings and collision handling.
  • Jump & Ground Detection: Jumping behaviour became inconsistent after certain collision interactions. I investigated ground detection and Rigidbody-based jump behaviour.
  • Obstacle Spawn Management: Excessive obstacle spawning affected gameplay readability. I worked on the spawning logic to improve obstacle distribution and gameplay balance.
