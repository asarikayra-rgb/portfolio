The Problem / Goal

Make a runner that feels fast on day one but never feels cheap. Goal: readable obstacles, rising challenge, smooth mobile controls.

My role: Game design, C# gameplay scripting, UI/UX, monetization flow.

Key Features

Swappable obstaclesModular obstacle sets, easy to mix and rebalance.
Difficulty curveSpeed and density ramp gradually with score.
UI/UX flowClear menus, retry in one tap.
MonetizationNon-intrusive reward flow.

Design Highlights

  • Obstacles are telegraphed early so every death feels fair.
  • Speed increases in steps, giving players time to adapt.
  • Controls tuned separately for touch and keyboard.
