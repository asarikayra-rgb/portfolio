The Problem / Goal

Transform environmental restoration into an interactive puzzle experience where players solve challenges to heal polluted environments.

The goal was to create a top-down 2D adventure where puzzle-solving directly impacts the game world, making environmental recovery visible and rewarding.

My Role: Game Design, Tilemap Level Design, C# Gameplay Programming, NPC Interaction, Quest Systems, Object Interactions and Puzzle Mechanics.

Playtest & Iteration

During development, I encountered several technical challenges while building the interaction, physics and puzzle systems.

  • Tilemap Collision Issues: The player was unable to cross certain water areas. I investigated the Player BoxCollider2D trigger settings to resolve the interaction issue.
  • Object Pickup & Drop System: Initially, only one stone could be picked up correctly. I updated the StonePickup script, introduced object tags and improved Rigidbody2D handling while carrying objects.
  • Puzzle Trigger Logic: Restoration needed to activate only when the correct puzzle object was placed. I added separate handling for Stone and WaterBlock objects and connected the WaterBlock drop interaction to PuzzleManager.RestoreGarden().
  • NPC & Quest Progression: The initial mission flow required clear interaction between the player and NPC. I structured the progression around talking to Lisa, removing a barrier stone, reaching the river and returning to the NPC.
  • Gameplay Interaction Debugging: I worked with colliders, tags and interaction conditions to improve how objects responded to player actions.
